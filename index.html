Every Book Has a History

Every old book has a history of its own. A set of Gibbon might have filled a country parson's shelf, a battered Plutarch might have been a schoolboy's prize in 1870, and a well-thumbed Dickens might have been read aloud by the fire for decades. At Aliceararebooks we go looking for these books and give them a second life.

Our focus is history and the classics: the great narratives of empire, war and exploration, the novels and poetry that have outlasted their authors, and the Greek and Roman writers everyone else built on. We dig through estate sales, country auctions and dusty boxes for early printings, fine bindings and illustrated editions.

Every book is inspected and described honestly. Then it's packed carefully, like the small piece of history it is, and sent to a new home.
